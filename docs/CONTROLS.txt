TS4 ACCESS CONTROLS

Each line gives a shortcut and its action. Start with Scanner and Menus.
Build controls are on by default; you can turn them off in settings.

SCANNER

Turn Scanner mode on in settings or press Ctrl+Alt+Space.
Keyboard navigation and the scanner feature must also be enabled.
Wait for "Scanner". Use these keys without holding Ctrl or Alt:

Up: Previous item in the current list.
Down: Next item in the current list.
Right: Enter a category or subcategory. On an item, repeat it.
Left or Backspace: Go back one level.
Enter: Enter a category or open a Sim or object's interactions.
Space: Repeat speech.
Escape: Normal game/menu behavior; it does not turn Scanner mode off.

Hold Up or Down to repeat. Right does not repeatedly enter groups.
Your place is remembered when you return to a group.
In Live mode, the camera follows the active Sim and arrows do not pan it.
At the hospital (Get to Work doctor career), patients are named with their
visit stage, for example: Mateo Lewis, patient, waiting.

Extra scanner shortcuts:
Ctrl+Alt+Space: Turn Scanner mode on or off.
Ctrl+Alt+S: Refresh the scanner.
Ctrl+Alt+W: Read your location.

MENUS AND DIALOGS

These controls take over when a menu opens. The scanner resumes afterward.

Simplified menus is on by default. Up and Down move through options one
at a time, including lists arranged in several columns. CAS and Stories
keep their existing controls. Turn Simplified menus off in accessibility
settings to restore the original menu navigation.

Tab: Next control. From the world, enter the HUD controls.
Shift+Tab: Previous control while browsing a menu or HUD panel.
In Live mode with menus closed, Shift+Tab toggles first-person view.
Arrow keys: Navigate controls or adjust values. Hold to repeat.
Enter: Activate or confirm, once per press.
Space: Select or toggle.
Escape: Close or cancel.
Backspace: Return from a picker footer button to the item list.
Ctrl+Alt+R: Read the current control or stop speech.

In text fields, arrows edit text. Tab or Shift+Tab leaves the field.
In purchase lists, Left and Right change quantity. Enter on an item
focuses Done; another Enter confirms. Tab also reaches Done.
In cooking and drink recipe lists, Enter chooses the focused recipe.
If it opens serving sizes, press Down to a size, then Enter to cook.
In Sim pickers, Space or Enter selects; Tab reaches confirmation.
With Simplified menus on, Enter confirms an enabled single-choice row.
For multiple choices, Space toggles a row and reads the selection count;
use Done to confirm. The game keeps its minimum and maximum selections.
In Load Game, each save is one choice; Enter uses its Play action.

In the Sim's inventory panel, Enter on an item opens an item menu.
Item actions opens the game's usual menu for that item. Sell rows show
the price: Sell for, or for a stack Sell one, Sell all (asks first) and
Choose how many. Choose how many opens the game's sell window: Left and
Right set the amount, then choose Sell. Items the game will not let you
sell say so. With the panel's multiselect on, Enter selects as usual.

In Household Split and Merge, Space selects a Sim, then Enter on Transfer
Sim(s) moves the selection to the other household. The amount field comes
after the two Transfer Funds buttons and before Done; it reads its current
number. Enter edits it, Ctrl+A selects the number, and Tab leaves editing.
Choose a Transfer Funds button to apply the amount in that direction.
Each Transfer Funds and Transfer Sim(s) button says which household it
moves from and to, and the funds buttons and amount say both balances.
Each Sim in the lists says which household it is in.
Done confirms the household changes; Escape cancels the dialog.
Businesses in the accessibility menu lists each business your household
owns, including mod businesses such as the WickedWhims strip club: open or
closed, stars, business funds, today's sales, costs and profit, markup and
employees. Enter opens the game's business panel. Amounts marked with a
plus or minus sign say plus or minus.
In a business's Manage Employees window, each Sim available for hire is
its own item: arrow to a Sim and press Enter to hire. Current employees
have Promote, Demote and Fire buttons after their name.
In a business's Transfer Funds dialog (Get to Work), the two lists say
From and To, and Confirm says the amount and both ends. In the move
dialog, each furnishing choice says which home it is for.

NEW GAME: GUIDED CREATE A SIM (TEST VERSION)

When Create a Sim opens for a new family (after the questionnaire choice),
a Create your Sim window offers a step-by-step guide. Start opens the
name step. Type the name opens the name boxes: type the first name, press
Tab, type the last name, then press Enter (Escape cancels). Random name
lets the game pick. Skip, or Escape on the first window, uses the full
Create a Sim screen. Then come age, gender, traits and aspiration, each a
window of buttons. Traits: Add a trait chooses a group, then a trait
(traits that clash with your choices are left out). Remove a trait takes
one off, and Next moves on. Aspiration: choose a category, then an
aspiration. The Your Sim window reads the name, traits and aspiration and
lets you change any of them before Done. Voice and walk style (optional)
lets you pick a voice, its pitch (Lowest to Highest) and a walk style;
skip it if you like. Escape in a step goes back one
step. After Done, Find a home saves your household and opens the world
map. If Seasons is installed, choose a season first. Then choose a world
(each says how many homes you can afford) and a home (cheapest first,
with bedrooms, bathrooms and price). Choose Furnished or Unfurnished:
each says what money is left. The guide then buys the home in the game's
own move-in window, and the game loads your lot. Escape on the world
list closes the guide and leaves the map to Tab browsing.
Before Find a home, Add another Sim adds a Sim to the household and runs
the same steps for them, then asks how they are related to the others
(for example Spouse, Parent or Sibling). Set relationships changes those
later, and Change another Sim goes back to any Sim's summary. Homes list
houses first, cheapest first; empty lots, where you build the house
yourself, come last.

NEW GAME: SEASON AND HOME

If Seasons is installed, the season screen has four choices: Spring,
Summer, Fall and Winter. Tab and Shift+Tab move between them. Space
selects a season without leaving the screen. Enter chooses the focused
season and continues to the world map.

On the world and neighborhood maps, Tab enters keyboard browsing. Use
Tab or the arrow keys to browse and Enter to select. Lot descriptions
include occupancy and affordability.
After choosing a house, Tab to Confirm and press Enter to open its
move-in dialog. Enter opens the furnishing choice, arrows browse
the options, and Enter selects one. Tab reaches Purchase Home. Listen to
the furnishing choice and remaining funds before pressing Enter to buy.
Escape cancels the dialog.

GENERAL SHORTCUTS

Close text fields before using these shortcuts. Most actions also require
closing menus. Day and time can be read while a menu is open. The phone
shortcut works from the call alert or HUD panels; close dialogs and pickers first.

Ctrl+Alt+H: Open the accessibility menu.
Choose Keyboard shortcuts in that menu to hear the controls.
Ctrl+Shift+N: Read needs.
Ctrl+Alt+L: Turn diagnostic logging on or off (TS4_Access.log next to the mod).
Ctrl+Alt+V: Running events. Enter on one to send all guests home and end it,
end it, or send all guests home. Your household is never sent away.
Ctrl+Alt+M: Milestones of the active Sim (Growing Together). Enter on a
category reads its milestones.
Ctrl+Alt+K: Character values of the active Sim (Parenthood).
Ctrl+Alt+C: Read the current day and time, including when the game is paused,
then your household funds.
Ctrl+Alt+E: Read the mood and its description, then every moodlet with
its effect, time left, reason and description, all at once.
Ctrl+Alt+G: Read wants and fears.
Ctrl+Alt+Q: Open the action queue. Each row is something the active Sim is
doing or waiting to do, with who chose it. Enter cancels that action.
Ctrl+Alt+P: Open a waiting call or text. With Simplified menus on and
nothing waiting, open the phone action list.
Ctrl+Alt+1 through Ctrl+Alt+8: Switch household Sim.
M: Open travel. With Simplified menus on, choose Go alone or Choose who
comes. Uses destination lists when Travel list is enabled.

The accessibility menu also opens panels such as skills, career,
inventory, relationships and calendar. It includes self interactions,
the action queue, events, day and time, household Sims and funds, known contacts and settings.
With Growing Together installed, Milestones (also Ctrl+Alt+M) lists the active Sim's milestone
categories; Enter on one reads each milestone, whether and at what age it
was reached, and the game's text or hint. With Parenthood installed,
Character values (also Ctrl+Alt+K) reads each value on its scale, the trait it leads to at
age up, and its description.

TS4 ACCESS PLUS (ALT KEYS)

By Michael Maslo, contributed through LeonianUniverse and merged into TS4 Access.
Hold Alt (not Ctrl) and press a letter. Do not also install the separate
TS4_Access_Plus.ts4script.

Alt+A: Access Plus menu, every feature and the Plus settings.
Alt+N: Needs with percentages, lowest first.
Alt+T: Day, time, game speed, when work or school starts, money and bills.
Alt+Q: The action queue (the same as Ctrl+Alt+Q).
Alt+R: Rooms of the house and what is in them. Enter on a thing opens its
actions.
Alt+W: Which room your Sim is in, and which floor.
Alt+L: The last 30 notifications, newest first.
Alt+C: Create and edit Sims without Create a Sim: start a brand-new family
on an empty lot or a house, add a Sim, change a name, traits or aspiration.
Alt+B: Build helper, in Build mode: build my house (one or two floors),
put a roof on, build a room, draw a wall, doors and windows, decorate,
furnish, sell things in a room, name rooms, and an accessible catalog.
In Build mode, Alt+arrows move a drawing cursor 1 meter (Shift+Alt+arrows
5 meters), Alt+S marks the start, Alt+D draws, Alt+X says where the cursor
is, Alt+Z undoes.
The build helper moves the real mouse for the game's own tools: leave the
mouse alone and keep the game window in front while it works.
Automatic messages (each can be switched off in Alt+A, Settings): low needs,
work and school in the next hour, bills, game speed changes, Build or Live
mode, and an action given while the game is paused.
Guides: docs/plus in the source kit.

OPTIONAL NUMPAD

Enable Numpad navigation in settings and turn NumLock on.
These keys navigate menus and panels, not the world scanner.

Numpad 4: Previous control.
Numpad 6: Next control.
Numpad 5: Activate.
Numpad 8: Read the current control or stop speech.
Numpad decimal: Back or close.

OPTIONAL CTRL+ALT SCANNER NAVIGATION

You can browse without Scanner mode by holding Ctrl+Alt with these keys.
You do not need this alternative when using ordinary scanner keys.

Up or Down: Previous or next item.
Right: Enter a group, or repeat an individual item.
Left or Backspace: Go back one level.
Enter: Enter a group or open interactions.
Page Up or Page Down: Jump to the previous or next subcategory.
R: Repeat speech.
S: Refresh the scanner.
W: Read your location.

While a dialog is open, Ctrl+Alt+arrows navigate its controls, Enter
activates, Backspace goes back or closes when allowed, and H reads help.
Scanner refresh and subcategory jumps do not operate behind dialogs.
On layouts with AltGr, use left Ctrl and left Alt for these shortcuts.
If Ctrl+Alt+arrows rotates your screen, disable the competing rotation
shortcut in your graphics software.

BUILD: PLACING OBJECTS

Build controls are on by default. Press F2 to enter Build mode.
If you previously turned Build controls off, enable them in settings first.
Entering Build mode turns off a Live lot grid.
This places objects from the Build catalog. It does not build walls,
floors, roofs, rooms or stairs; see README.txt for what is not covered.

1. Open the Build catalog and choose an object with Enter.
2. Press Ctrl+Alt+B. You hear "Build placement on", the chosen object and
   which side of the lot the street is on. The catalog stays open for the
   game; Tab returns to it and Escape comes back.
3. Move with the plain arrow keys, one tile at a time. Up is north.
   Each tile says what stands on it, walls and doors around it, when you
   enter another room or go outside, and whether the chosen object fits
   there at its current facing.
4. Press R to turn the object (Shift+R turns it the other way).
5. Press Enter to buy it and place it exactly on this tile.
   If it does not fit, you hear why and the nearest tile where it fits,
   for example "It fits 2 east, facing north". Press Enter again to place
   it there, or move to cancel.
After placing, you hear its facing and the tiles it covers, and the cursor
moves onto it.

While Build placement is on:
Arrow keys: Move one tile. Hold to repeat.
Page Up / Page Down: Go up or down a floor.
Enter: Place the chosen object here (or take the offered spot).
R / Shift+R: Turn the chosen object.
Space: Read this tile again.
1: What stands here, with anything on it and free spots on surfaces.
2: Floor, inside or outside.
3: Walls and doors on all four sides.
4: Objects within 3 tiles, with directions.
5: The chosen object, its facing and whether it fits here.
6: Your position on the lot, the street side and the front door.
Home: Jump to the last placed object.
Delete: Sell the object here. Press twice to confirm.
Escape or Backspace: Turn Build placement off.
Tab: Go back to the Build catalog. Escape there returns to placement.
In Build mode, Tab moves through the catalog in this order: filters (when a
category is open), search, the Build/Buy/Function tabs, categories, then the
products and their colours. EA's tool buttons, such as undo, are left out;
use the game's own hotkeys for them.
The Ctrl+Alt keys still work: Ctrl+Alt+arrows move, Ctrl+Alt+Enter
places, Ctrl+Alt+Z undoes the last placement and gets the money back,
Ctrl+Alt+X sells, and Ctrl+Alt+left or right bracket turns the object you
last placed.

To place on a desk, table or counter, move onto it. Its free spots are
read out, and objects such as computers and lamps go into a free spot.
Objects that hang on walls or ceilings are not placed from the cursor yet.
Scanner mode does not need to be turned off in Build mode.
Press F1 to return to Live mode. This turns Build placement off.
Undo with Ctrl+Alt+Z covers placements made this way. The game's own
Build undo does not.
When objects share a tile, selling targets a slotted object before the
surface beneath it. Sale and undo refuse an object that contains another
object or is being used; remove its contents or finish the interaction first.

LOT GRID IN LIVE MODE

Enable Build controls and turn Scanner mode off before using the grid in
Live mode.

Ctrl+Alt+T: Turn the grid on or off.

While the grid is active, hold Ctrl+Alt with:
Arrow keys: Move the sampled point one metre.
Page Up: Go up a floor.
Page Down: Go down a floor.
Enter, R, W or S: Describe the sampled point.
Backspace: Exit the grid.

In Live mode the grid only reads the lot. It does not place objects.
