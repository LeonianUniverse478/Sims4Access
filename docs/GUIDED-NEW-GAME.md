# Guided new game

The guide takes a new player from New Game to playing on their own lot. Every
step is a window of buttons: arrow to a button and press Enter. Escape in a step
goes back one step. Appearance stays as the game makes it; you can change it
later in Create a Sim.

## Starting

1. On the main menu, choose New Game.
2. The game asks whether to answer its questionnaire. Choose either.
3. A **Create your Sim** window appears. Choose **Start**, or **Skip the guide
   and use the full Create a Sim screen**. Escape on this first window also
   skips the guide.

## Your Sim

- **Step 1: Name.** The window says your Sim's current name.
  - **Type the name** opens the game's name boxes: type the first name, press
    Tab, type the last name, then press Enter. Escape cancels.
  - **Random name** lets the game pick one.
  - **Keep this name** moves on.
- **Step 2: Age.** Infant to Elder. The current age says "(now)". Young Adult is
  a good first choice.
- **Step 3: Gender.** Female or Male.
- **Step 4: Traits.** The window says which traits are chosen and how many your
  Sim can have.
  - **Add a trait** asks for a kind of trait, then a trait. Traits your Sim
    already has, or that clash with them, are left out.
  - **Remove a trait** takes one off.
  - **Next: aspiration** moves on.
- **Step 5: Aspiration.** Choose a category, then an aspiration.
- **Your Sim.** A summary of the name, traits and aspiration, with **Done** and
  a button to change each part.
- **Voice and walk style (optional).** From the summary: a voice, the voice's
  pitch (Lowest to Highest) and a walk style. The voice and pitch lists stay
  open so you can try each one. Children and younger have no walk style. You
  never have to visit this to continue.

## Your household

After **Done**, the **Your Sim is ready** window offers:

- **Find a home** (see below).
- **Add another Sim.** A new Sim is added and goes through the same steps. After
  their summary, the guide asks how they are related to each of the others, such
  as Spouse, Parent or Sibling.
- **Set relationships** and **Change another Sim**, once there are two or more
  Sims.
- **Change how my Sims look first.** The guide closes and Create a Sim is yours.
  Its Play button also leads to choosing a home.

A household needs at least one young adult, adult or elder.

## Finding a home

1. **Your household.** The window says the household's name. **Save and find a
   home** saves the household to your library and opens the world map. This
   takes a moment.
2. **Choose a season.** Only with the Seasons pack. Spring is a good first
   choice.
3. **Choose a world.** Each world says how many houses your household can
   afford and how many empty lots it has. Vacation worlds are not listed.
   Escape here closes the guide and leaves the map to Tab browsing.
4. **Choose a home.** Houses come first, cheapest first, each with its
   neighborhood, bedrooms, bathrooms and price. Homes you cannot afford say "Too
   expensive". Empty lots come last and say "Empty lot with no house, you build
   it yourself".
5. **Furnished or Unfurnished.** Each says what money would be left. Furnished
   comes with furniture, so your Sims can start right away.
6. The guide buys the home in the game's own move-in window, and the game loads
   your lot. If the lot has not loaded after about 25 seconds, a **Play** button
   appears.

## If something goes wrong

If the guide runs into a problem, it says "The guide ran into a problem and
closed", and you can carry on with the game's own screens. Send
`TS4_Access.log` from your Mods folder: it records each guide step.
