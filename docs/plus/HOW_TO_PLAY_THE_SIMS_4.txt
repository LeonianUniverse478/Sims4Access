HOW TO PLAY THE SIMS 4 WITH A SCREEN READER
A beginner's guide, written for you by Claude, October 2026.
Uses two mods: TS4 Access (by another author) and TS4 Access Plus (my add-on).

Read one part at a time. You do not need to learn it all at once.


PART 1. WHAT THE GAME IS

The Sims 4 is a life game. There is no story you must finish and no way to
"lose". You look after a family of people called Sims. You tell them what to
do: eat, sleep, shower, go to work, make friends, learn skills, fall in love.
Time keeps moving, like a real day, but much faster.

Your goals are whatever you like. Common goals:
- Get your Sim a job and promotions, and earn money.
- Learn skills, like cooking or painting.
- Make friends, find a partner, start a family.
- Complete the Sim's aspiration. That is a life goal with steps, for
  example "Become friends with 10 Sims".

Sims have six NEEDS. They go down over time, and you must refill them:
- Hunger: eat. Use the fridge (Quick Meal or Cook) or eat a served plate.
- Bladder: use the toilet.
- Energy: sleep in a bed. A full night is about 8 game hours.
- Hygiene: shower or take a bath.
- Fun: watch TV, play on the computer, read a book.
- Social: talk to other Sims.
If a need gets very low, the Sim gets unhappy, and a Sim with no energy
falls asleep on the floor. A Sim who is very hungry for too long can die,
so keep an eye on hunger.

Sims also have a MOOD, like Happy, Sad, Angry, Tense or Inspired. Moods come
from "moodlets", small feelings caused by what happens. A happy Sim learns
faster and works better.


PART 2. BEFORE YOU START THE GAME

1. Close the game if it is open.
2. You do not need to run TS4AccessHelper.exe every time. Run it only
   after a Sims 4 update. It turns the mod settings back on. Mods are on
   right now.
3. Start JAWS first, then start The Sims 4 from Steam.
4. On the main menu, TS4 Access reads the buttons. Use arrows and Enter.


PART 3. GETTING A FAMILY TO PLAY

You already have a saved game (it was saved on September 29). To continue:
choose Load Game on the main menu, then choose the save and press Enter.

Other ways to get a family:
- Play a family that comes with the game. The game includes ready-made
  families, for example the Goths and the Pancakes in Willow Creek.
  From inside a game, open the game menu with Escape and choose
  Manage Worlds. Press Tab to browse the map, choose a house with a family,
  and choose Play.
- Start a brand-new family of your own, with your own home: load any
  save, press Alt+C and choose "Start a brand-new family". Full steps are
  in MAKE_YOUR_OWN_FAMILY_AND_HOUSE.txt.
- Make your own Sims. The game's own Create a Sim screen mostly needs a
  mouse. My add-on gets around that: load any family, then press Alt+C and
  choose "Add a new Sim to this household". You choose age, gender, name and
  traits by keyboard. The game gives the Sim a random look.
  You can also change the name, traits and aspiration of a Sim you already
  have. Note: a household can hold at most 8 Sims.


PART 4. THE TWO MOST IMPORTANT TOOLS

A. THE SCANNER (from TS4 Access): finding things and telling Sims what to do

In The Sims you tell a Sim what to do by choosing an object or another Sim
and picking an action from its menu. For example: fridge, then "Quick Meal".

1. Turn Scanner mode on with Ctrl+Alt+Space. Wait to hear "Scanner".
   (It stays on. You only need to do this once.)
2. Up and Down arrows move through groups: Sims, Appliances, Plumbing,
   Comfort and so on. Each item says its distance and direction.
3. Right arrow enters a group. Up and Down move through its items.
4. Enter on an item opens its action menu. Up and Down to choose an action,
   Enter to do it.
5. Left arrow or Backspace goes back out.

Example, to eat: Scanner, find Appliances, Right arrow, find the
Refrigerator, Enter, choose "Quick Meal" or "Cook", Enter.
Example, to sleep: Scanner, Comfort, Right, the bed, Enter, "Sleep".
Example, to chat: Scanner, Sims, Right, a Sim's name, Enter, then choose
a friendly action such as "Get to Know" or a chat.

The scanner only lists things your Sim can use right now.

B. THE ACCESS PLUS KEYS (my add-on): information and help

Hold Alt and press a letter:
  Alt+A  The Access Plus menu. Lists every feature. Start here if you forget.
  Alt+N  Needs. Reads all six needs, lowest first, with a percentage.
  Alt+T  Time. Day, time, game speed, when work or school starts,
         household money, and unpaid bills.
  Alt+Q  Action queue. Lists what your Sim is doing and what is waiting.
         Choose an action to cancel it, or choose "Cancel everything".
  Alt+R  Rooms. Lists the rooms of the house: kitchen, bathroom, bedroom
         and so on, with their size and direction. Choose a room to hear
         everything in it. Choose an object to open its action menu.
  Alt+W  Where am I. Which room your Sim is in, and which floor.
  Alt+L  Last notifications. The pop-up messages, newest first, such as
         "promoted", "bills have arrived", or a skill level gained.
  Alt+C  Create and edit Sims.
  Alt+B  Build helper (only in Build mode, F2). See part 9.

Typing in an Access Plus text box: just type, then press Enter. Escape
cancels. When a name is already filled in, Enter on its own keeps it.

Automatic warnings (Alt+A, then Settings, turns each one on or off):
- Need warnings: you hear for example "Bladder low", or "Bella: Hunger very
  low". These are the most important sounds in the game. Act on them.
- Work reminders: "Bella has work in 1 hour, at 9 AM".
- Bills: "Bills have arrived: 450 simoleons. Pay them at the mailbox."

Other useful TS4 Access keys:
  Ctrl+Alt+H      TS4 Access menu: skills, career, relationships, inventory,
                  calendar, phone, settings.
  Ctrl+Shift+N    Needs (the TS4 Access version).
  Ctrl+Alt+E      Mood and moodlets.
  Ctrl+Alt+G      Wants and fears.
  Ctrl+Alt+P      Phone: answer a call or text, or open phone actions.
  Ctrl+Alt+1 to 8 Switch to another Sim in your household.
  Ctrl+Alt+W      Read your location in the scanner.
  M               Travel to another place.
  Escape          Game menu (Save, Load, Options, Quit).


PART 5. GAME SPEED

The game runs in real time but much faster than real life.
- P pauses the game. Pause whenever you need to think. Nothing happens while
  paused, and you can still give orders.
- 1 is normal speed, 2 is fast, 3 is ultra fast.
Alt+T tells you the current speed.
A good habit: give orders while paused, then press 1 or 2 to let them happen.


PART 6. A GOOD FIRST DAY (A SIMPLE ROUTINE)

1. Load your save. Press P to pause. Press Alt+T to hear the time.
2. Press Alt+N to check needs. Fix the lowest one first.
3. Get a job:
   Ctrl+Alt+P opens the phone actions. Look for the career category and
   "Find a Job". Pick a career. Good first careers: Culinary (cooking),
   Painter, Writer, Tech Guru.
   Or use the computer: Scanner, the computer, Enter, look for "Find a Job".
4. Your Sim goes to work by themselves when work starts. You hear the
   reminder an hour before. Make sure needs are good before work.
5. While the Sim is home and free: learn a skill that helps the job.
   Writer: write on the computer. Culinary: cook at the fridge or stove.
   Painter: paint at an easel.
6. In the evening: eat, have fun, chat with someone, shower.
7. Go to bed around 10 or 11 PM so the Sim can get up for work.
   At night, press 3 for ultra fast speed while everyone sleeps.
8. Save often: Escape, then Save.

Money: you start with some simoleons (the money). Every few days bills
arrive in the mailbox. Pay them at the mailbox (Scanner, the mailbox, Enter,
Pay Bills). If you do not pay, the power and water get cut off.


PART 7. FRIENDS AND RELATIONSHIPS

Choose another Sim with the scanner and press Enter. The menu has groups
such as Friendly, Romance, Mean, Funny. Friendly actions build friendship.
Start with "Introduce Yourself" or "Get to Know", then chat, tell jokes,
compliment. Ctrl+Alt+H, then relationships, shows how friendly you are with
everyone.


PART 8. SKILLS, ASPIRATIONS, CAREERS

- Skills go from level 1 to 10. You hear when your Sim gains a level, and you
  can read it again with Alt+L. Ctrl+Alt+H, then skills, lists them.
- Your Sim's aspiration has steps. Ctrl+Alt+H, then aspirations, shows the
  current goals. Finishing them gives rewards.
- Careers: go to work every day, keep the mood good, and do the daily task
  (Ctrl+Alt+H, then career, tells you what helps). This leads to promotions
  and more money.


PART 9. BUYING THINGS AND BUILDING (Alt+B)

F2 opens Build mode. F1 goes back to Live mode. In Build mode, press Alt+B
for the build helper. Everything below is in that menu.

Positions on your lot are given in meters: how far from the WEST edge,
and how far from the NORTH edge. "Describe the lot" tells you the lot size,
where every room is, and where your Sim stands.

A. ADD A WHOLE FURNISHED ROOM (the easiest way to build)
   1. Alt+B, Catalog, Furnished rooms, then a kind: Bathrooms, Bedrooms,
      Kitchens, Living rooms and more. Each has a name and a price.
   2. Press Enter on one you can afford.
   3. Alt+B, "Place the chosen furnished room". You get a list of free
      places, each saying whether the room will join the house. Press Enter.
   4. You hear the size of the new room and where it is.
   The new room comes with its own door, but it may face outside. Add a door
   into the house as in C below.

B. BUILD AN EMPTY ROOM
   1. In the game's catalog choose the Room Tool. The quickest way: Alt+B,
      Catalog, "Rooms and wall tools", Room Tool.
   2. Alt+B, "Build a room". Type the width (west to east) and depth (north
      to south), for example 4 5, and press Enter.
   3. Choose one of the free places offered. You hear when it is built.
   Rooms must stay 1 meter inside the edge of the lot.

C. DOORS AND WINDOWS
   1. Alt+B, Catalog, Doors (or Windows). Enter on one.
   2. Alt+B, "Put the chosen door or window on a wall".
   3. Choose the room, then the wall. Each wall says how long it is and how
      many doors and windows it already has.
   4. Choose the meter along the wall. Each one says "Free" or what is there.
   If the game refuses, you hear why, for example
   "Can't intersect other objects" (furniture is in the way).

D. FURNITURE
   1. Alt+B, Catalog, "Furniture and other objects", then a kind, such as
      Beds or Refrigerators. Enter on one.
   2. Alt+B, "Put the chosen object in a room". Choose the room.
   TS4 Access finds a free spot and tells you where it went and the price.
   (TS4 Access's own grid placement, in its CONTROLS.txt, still works too.)

E. PAINT
   Alt+B, Catalog, "Wall patterns" (or "Floor patterns"), Enter on one.
   Then Alt+B, "Paint the walls of a room" (or the floor). The whole room
   is painted.

F. MISTAKES
   Alt+B, "Undo the last build step". You get the money back.

Not done yet: roofs, stairs and second floors. In the base game a roof is
only for looks: Sims do not get wet, because there is no weather.
Moving to a bigger furnished house is still an option: phone actions, Move
Household.


PART 10. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

- IMPORTANT: Shift+Tab opens the Steam overlay on top of the game, and then
  the game gets no keys at all. If the game suddenly stops answering, press
  Shift+Tab once more to close the overlay. Claude can turn the overlay off
  for The Sims 4 if you want; just ask.

- A Sim is stuck or will not do something: Alt+Q and cancel the action, then
  try again.
- You hear nothing when pressing an Alt key: make sure the game window is in
  front, and that a family is loaded.
- The game reads too much or jumps around: that is TS4 Access's reader.
  Ctrl+Alt+R reads the current thing again, or stops speech.
- After a Sims 4 update the mods may be off: close the game, run
  TS4AccessHelper.exe, choose Enable Script Mods, start the game again.
- Tell me what happened. My add-on writes a log file I can read:
  Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 4\Mods\TS4_Access_Plus.log
