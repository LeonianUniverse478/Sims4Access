TS4 ACCESS PLUS - CHANGELOG

0.7.1 - October 7, 2026 (NOT yet tested in the game)
- FIX for "I stopped building at the bedroom 1, because the walls did
  not close" (your sister, two-floor house, first upstairs room).
  The mod now waits up to 5 seconds for a new room to show up (slower
  computers take longer, upstairs especially). If the walls still did
  not close, it takes them back with Undo and draws the room again,
  up to 4 tries, instead of stopping.
- The log file is now ALSO saved in your Windows user folder:
  C:\Users\(your name)\TS4_Access_Plus.log  (outside OneDrive), so it
  can always be found. If the Mods folder copy cannot be written, that
  copy says why.

0.7.0 - October 7, 2026 (tested live by Claude, game NOT saved)
TWO-FLOOR HOUSES WITH STAIRS (Alt+B, Build my house)
- Three new choices in Build my house, for lots that are too small for a
  one-floor house. Each is 10 by 11 meters with stairs in the hallway:
    Two floors, 3 bedrooms (downstairs living room, kitchen, dining room,
      bathroom; upstairs 3 bedrooms and a bathroom)
    Two floors, 4 bedrooms (a bedroom downstairs instead of the dining room)
    Two floors, 2 bedrooms (a study upstairs instead of bedroom 3)
  TESTED: the 3 bedroom house was built with all 10 rooms, the stairs
  and all 9 doors, and a Sim can walk to every room, upstairs too.
  It cost about 9,900 simoleons, so it needs about 10,000.
- Put a roof on my house now puts the roof on the TOP floor (TESTED).
- Decorate a room and Furnish a room work on upstairs rooms (TESTED:
  upstairs floor painted, upstairs bedroom furnished).
- The mod switches floors itself (Page Up / Page Down). After building
  you are back on the ground floor.
- FIX for "I stopped building at the living room, because it came out
  a different size": the mouse could land on the catalog panel or the
  Cancel bar at the bottom of the screen, or on something already
  standing on the lot. The mod now never points at the bottom panels.
  If it still happens, start on an EMPTY lot and send Claude the file
  TS4_Access_Plus.log from the Mods folder.

0.6.0 - October 6, 2026 (tested live by Claude, game NOT saved)
NEW IN THE BUILD HELPER (Alt+B in Build mode)
- Put a roof on my house (TESTED: a gabled roof over the whole 11 by 11
  meter house). Choose a roof style from the list. Roofs cost nothing.
- Draw with a cursor, like sighted players (TESTED: wall drawn, undo).
  Alt+arrows move a cursor 1 meter (Shift+Alt+arrows 5 meters). You hear
  where it is, which rooms are around it, and if it is on a wall.
  Alt+S marks the start, Alt+D draws from the start to the cursor,
  Alt+X says where you are, Alt+Z takes back the last thing.
  Tools (Alt+B, Choose the drawing tool): Wall, Room, Roof.
- Fences do not work yet (the game refused them). Coming later.

0.5.0 - October 6, 2026 (tested live by Claude, game NOT saved)
NEW IN THE BUILD HELPER (Alt+B in Build mode)
- Decorate a room (TESTED: walls and floor painted): choose the room, then Walls or Floor, then a paint,
  wallpaper or floor from a list with prices. The whole room is done at
  once and you hear what it cost. The first color of each pattern is used
  for now. Choosing other colors comes later.
- Sell things in a room (TESTED: sold 2 things, got 170 back): lists everything in the room with what it sells
  for. Check the ones you want to sell (Space or Enter), choose OK, then
  say Yes. You get the money back, the same as the TS4 Access X key.
  Things that have something on them, or that someone is using, cannot be
  sold until that is moved.
NEXT: moving and turning furniture, choosing colors, stairs.

0.3.0 - October 6, 2026 (tested live by Claude, game NOT saved, your save untouched)

NEW: START A BRAND-NEW FAMILY (Alt+C, first choice)
- Make 1 to 8 Sims: family name, then for each Sim age, gender, first name,
  traits, aspiration, and how they are related (married, child, parent,
  brother or sister, or roommate).
- Choose an empty lot to build on, or a ready-made house. Each says its
  town, price and the money left. The family gets 20,000 simoleons.
- The game moves the family in and switches to playing them. The family you
  were playing stays in the town.
- Tested: a married couple with a daughter moved onto an empty lot in
  Newcrest (Comfy Cubby). A single Sim moved into a house (Nookstone).
  Marriage and parents were set correctly.
- Full steps: MAKE_YOUR_OWN_FAMILY_AND_HOUSE.txt

FIXES
- ARROW KEYS: JAWS was catching the arrow keys, so Up and Down did nothing
  in the game's lists. New JAWS key file TS4_x64.jkm (same fix as
  Fallout 4) passes them to the game. Tested: arrows work now.
- New Sims got extra random traits from the game. Now they get exactly the
  traits you choose (also for "Add a new Sim to this household").
- Trait lists: "Done" moved to the END of the list, so pressing Enter on the
  first row never finishes by accident.
- The game's tutorial aspirations are no longer offered.

KNOWN
- TS4 Access says "Needs - O" when a list closes. Harmless.
- Lot prices are "about": within 100 simoleons.
- Stairs, second floors and roofs: next.
