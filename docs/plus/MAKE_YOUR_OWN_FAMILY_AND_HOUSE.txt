MAKE YOUR OWN FAMILY AND BUILD YOUR OWN HOUSE
Step by step, written for you by Claude, October 6, 2026.
TS4 Access Plus version 0.4.0.

Do one part at a time. If you get stuck anywhere, alt-tab to Claude and
say "stuck", and what you heard last. Claude reads the game's log and tells
you what to press next.


BEFORE YOU START: TWO GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

1. Arrow keys now work in all the game's lists. JAWS was catching the arrows
   before the game got them. Claude added a JAWS key file for The Sims 4
   (TS4_x64.jkm), the same fix as Fallout 4. You do not need Insert+3.
2. Your old saved game was backed up first, so nothing can be lost.

How every list works (you will use this all the time):
- Up and Down arrows move through the choices. JAWS reads each one.
- Enter chooses.
- Escape goes back or cancels.
- In a typing box: just type, then press Enter.


PART 1. START THE GAME AND LOAD YOUR SAVE

The game can only make a new family from inside a game that is already
loaded. So first you load your old save. Your old Sim (michael) just stays
in the town. You will not play him any more unless you want to.

1. Start JAWS first, then start The Sims 4 from Steam.
2. Wait for the main menu. It can take a minute or two.
3. If a message about mods appears, press Enter to close it.
4. Choose Resume Game (or Load Game, then your save) and press Enter.
5. Wait until the game has loaded. You hear Sims or need warnings.
6. Press P to pause. You hear "Paused". Nothing happens while paused.


PART 2. MAKE YOUR FAMILY

Nothing changes in the game until the very end, when you say yes to
moving in. You can cancel at any time.

1. Press Alt+C. The list "Create and edit Sims" opens.
2. The first choice is "Start a brand-new family". Press Enter.
3. Choose "Start" and press Enter.
4. Type the family's last name, for example Maslo, and press Enter.

Now you make the first Sim. This one must be a grown up.

5. Age: Young Adult, Adult or Elder. Young Adult is the usual choice.
   Press Enter on one.
6. Gender: Female or Male. Press Enter on one.
7. Type the first name and press Enter.
8. Traits: personality. A grown-up Sim gets 3.
   Arrow down through the list. Each trait says what it does.
   Press Enter on one you like. The list comes back for the next one.
   Traits that do not go together are left out automatically.
   After 3 traits it goes on by itself.
   Good easy traits for a first game: Cheerful, Active, Creative,
   Good, Family-Oriented, Neat, Outgoing.
9. Aspiration: a life goal. Arrow down and press Enter on one.
   Escape skips it. Easy ones: Big Happy Family, Friend of the World,
   Bodybuilder, Painter Extraordinaire.
10. You hear for example "Mary Maslo, Young Adult female added."

The family menu opens. It has:
   Add another Sim
   Done, choose a home
   Hear the family
   Remove the last Sim
   Cancel the whole family

To add a husband, wife, child or roommate:
11. Choose "Add another Sim" and do steps 5 to 9 again.
    The second Sim can be any age: also Teen, Child or Toddler.
    Children and toddlers get 1 trait. Teens get 2.
12. One extra question: "family tie". Arrow down to choose:
    - "Spouse, married to ..." (husband or wife)
    - "Child of ..." (a son or daughter)
    - "Parent of ..."
    - "Brother or sister of ..."
    - "No family tie, a roommate"
    A child of a married parent is automatically the child of both.
13. Add more Sims the same way. A family can have up to 8.

The game gives each Sim a random look. You never see it, so it does not
matter. Sighted friends will just see a normal Sim.

If you close the family menu by mistake, nothing is lost: press Alt+C and
choose "Continue the new family".


PART 3. CHOOSE YOUR HOME

Your family gets 20,000 simoleons (the game's money), the same as a sighted
player gets.

1. In the family menu choose "Done, choose a home" and press Enter.
2. Two choices:
   - "Empty lots, build your own house". This is what you want.
   - "Houses ready to live in". A finished house, if you change your mind.
3. Press Enter on "Empty lots". You hear "Getting prices", then the list.
4. Each lot says its name, its town and its price, for example:
   "Comfy Cubby, Newcrest. About 1450 simoleons. 18550 simoleons left."
   Newcrest is a town made for building, with many empty lots.
   The cheapest lots are small. A small lot is fine for a first house and
   leaves more money for the house itself.
5. Press Enter on a lot.
6. It asks "Move in?" and repeats the family, the lot and the money.
   Choose "Yes, move in and play the ... family" and press Enter.
7. The game loads the new lot, like any trip. This takes about a minute.
8. You hear "Welcome home, ... family. You have ... simoleons. The lot is
   empty. It is ... by ... meters."
9. Press P to pause. Press Escape, choose Save, press Enter.
   Save right away, so your new family is kept.

Your family is now standing on an empty lot. Sims can wait outside for a
while, but they need a toilet, a bed and a fridge soon. Build next.


PART 4. BUILD YOUR HOUSE

Keep the game paused (P) while you build. You will hear "Paused".

GETTING INTO BUILD MODE
Your computer is a laptop. The F keys at the top need the Fn key held
down (bottom left, next to Ctrl). So:
  Fn+F2 = Build mode.   Fn+F1 = back to Live mode (normal play).
If Fn+F2 does nothing, tell Claude.

In Build mode press Alt+B. The build helper opens. Its first choices are:
  Build my house
  Door between two rooms
  Remove a door or window
  Furnish a room with the basics
  Name a room
  ...and the older tools (Catalog, Build a room, and so on).

THE EASY WAY: BUILD MY HOUSE (about 5 minutes)
1. Fn+F2, then Alt+B, then "Build my house", Enter.
2. Choose "Living room, kitchen, bathroom and 2 bedrooms", Enter.
   (Or "Choose the rooms yourself" to add rooms one at a time.)
3. You hear the plan. For your lot it is:
   - The house faces the street, with 2 meters of front yard.
   - At the front: the living room (7 by 5 meters) and the kitchen
     (4 by 5).
   - Behind them a hallway (2 meters wide) across the whole house.
   - At the back: bedroom 1, the bathroom, bedroom 2 (4 by 4 each,
     the bathroom 3 by 4).
   - Doors: ONE front door from the street into the living room. A door
     from the living room to the kitchen, from the living room to the
     hallway, and from the hallway into each bedroom and the bathroom.
     No room has a door to the outside except the living room.
   - The price: about 5,300 simoleons.
4. Choose "Build it" and press Enter. Then do not press any keys.
   You hear each room and door as it is built. At the end you hear
   "Your house is finished", the price, and the money left.
5. The rooms now have names: Living room, Kitchen, Hallway, Bedroom 1,
   Bathroom, Bedroom 2. Alt+R says them in Live mode.

A HOUSE WITH TWO FLOORS (when the lot is too small)
If you hear "does not fit on this lot", choose a two-floor house in
Build my house: "Two floors, 3 bedrooms" (or 4 bedrooms, or 2).
It is 10 by 11 meters and needs about 10,000 simoleons.
   - Downstairs at the front: living room (6 by 5) and kitchen (4 by 5).
   - A hallway across the house with the STAIRS in it. You step onto the
     stairs at the left end (seen from the street) and come out
     upstairs at the right end.
   - Downstairs at the back: dining room (7 by 4) and bathroom (3 by 4).
   - Upstairs at the front: bedroom 1 and bedroom 2 (5 by 5 each).
   - Upstairs hallway, then bedroom 3 (7 by 4) and the upstairs bathroom.
   - Every room has a door to its hallway.
The mod changes floors by itself. Afterwards the game shows the ground
floor again. Page Up and Page Down change floors yourself.
"Put a roof on my house" puts the roof on top of the second floor.

FURNISH EACH ROOM (about 1 minute per room)
6. Alt+B, "Furnish a room with the basics", Enter.
7. Choose a room, for example Kitchen.
8. Say what kind of room it is. The likely kind is first in the list.
   Each kind says what it buys and the price:
   - Bedroom for a couple: double bed and a lamp. About 785.
   - Toddler's bedroom: toddler bed and a lamp. About 210.
     (Use this for Shay.)
   - Bathroom: toilet, shower, sink and a lamp. About 830.
   - Kitchen: fridge, stove, 2 counters, trash can, lamp. About 1,185.
   - Living room: sofa, television and a lamp. About 795.
   - Hallway or other room: a lamp. About 60.
9. Press Enter. You hear "Done furnishing", what was bought and the price.
10. Do the same for every room.
11. The roof: Alt+B, "Put a roof on my house", Enter. Choose "Gabled Roof"
    and press Enter. Do not press keys for about 40 seconds. You hear
    "The roof is on". Roofs cost nothing.
The whole house with furniture costs about 9,100 simoleons. You will have
about 8,900 left. Sims cook at the counters, so keep both.

DO IT YOURSELF: YOUR OWN DOORS
- A door between two rooms: Alt+B, "Door between two rooms". Choose the
  first room. Then choose the other room (it says how many meters of
  wall they share), or "Outside, the ... wall" for a door to the yard.
  The one facing the street says "facing the street". The door goes in
  the middle of the shared wall.
  If you chose a door yourself in Alt+B, Catalog, Doors, that door is
  used. Otherwise a simple door for 90 simoleons.
- Remove a door: Alt+B, "Remove a door or window". Each one says where
  it is, for example "Door between the Hallway and the Bathroom", or
  "Door from the Kitchen to outside, east wall". Press Enter on it. You
  get the money back.
- Name a room: Alt+B, "Name a room", for example "Shay's room".
- Your own rooms one at a time: Alt+B, "Build a room" (as before).
- Draw like a sighted player: Alt+B, "Draw with a cursor, like sighted
  players". Alt+arrows move a cursor around the lot, 1 meter at a time
  (hold Shift too for 5 meters). Up is north, toward the street. You hear
  where it is and which rooms or walls are there. Alt+S marks the start
  corner, Alt+D draws from there to the cursor, Alt+X says where you are,
  Alt+Z takes back the last thing. Alt+B, "Choose the drawing tool":
  Wall (a straight line), Room or Roof (start and cursor are opposite
  corners). Alt+B, "Turn the drawing cursor off" when you are done.

MISTAKES
Alt+B, "Undo the last build step" takes back the last step, with a
refund. Press it again to go further back.

WHEN YOU ARE DONE
- Fn+F1 for Live mode. Alt+R lists your rooms.
- Save: Escape, Save, Enter.

Check your money often with Alt+T. Keep at least 1,000 simoleons for food
and the first bills.

Not done yet: ceiling lights (you get floor lamps instead), stairs and
second floors, and fences.


PART 5. YOUR FIRST DAY IN THE NEW HOUSE

The guide HOW_TO_PLAY_THE_SIMS_4.txt has everything about playing.
The short version:
- Alt+N needs, lowest first. Fix the lowest one.
- Alt+R rooms, choose a room, choose an object, choose an action.
  For example: Kitchen, the fridge, Quick Meal.
- To switch between your Sims: Ctrl+Alt+1, Ctrl+Alt+2 and so on.
- Find a job with the phone: Ctrl+Alt+P.
- P pauses. 1, 2, 3 are the speeds.
- Save often.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

- Arrows do nothing in a list: alt-tab out of the game and back in.
  That makes JAWS load the Sims key file. If it still does not work,
  restart JAWS once, then go back to the game.
- You hear "Load a game first": load your save first (Part 1).
- Anything else: alt-tab to Claude and say "stuck". Claude reads
  Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 4\Mods\TS4_Access_Plus.log
