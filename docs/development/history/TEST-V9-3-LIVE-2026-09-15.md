## Scanner changes under test

- Real-time alarm remains two seconds, including while paused.
- After 30 seconds without scanner input, skip world enumeration entirely.
  A subsequent action refreshes stale results before navigation/activation.
- Reject objects beyond 40 metres before affordance checks and metadata.
- Cache only definition classification, bounded at 1,024 entries with a
  30-second expiry; clear on zone load and explicit rescan.
- Continue checking hidden/inventory state, interactability, names and positions.
- Avoid a duplicate alarm scan immediately after a user-triggered scan.
- Refresh immediately when the active Sim or floor changes; ordinary walking
  retains the two-second bound rather than rescanning on every keypress.
- `a11y.scan stats` logs timings and counts without waking idle scanning.

Eight new behavioral checks pass, alongside the existing 133 checks. Main
and compatibility gates passed 61 and 19 targeted comparisons respectively;
14 Python modules match source. Live scanner timings are still pending.
