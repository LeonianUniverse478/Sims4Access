# TS4 Access: Build Mode Guide

**TS4 Access** lets you browse the Build catalog and place supported objects using the keyboard. You can furnish your home with items such as beds, cribs, tables, and appliances, and hear where they were placed and how much they cost!

**Build controls are enabled by default.** If you previously turned them off, open Settings from the accessibility menu and enable them again.

## **Placing an Object**

1. Press **F2** to enter Build Mode.
2. Browse the Build catalog and choose an object with **Enter**.
3. Press **Ctrl + Alt + B**. You hear **"Build placement on"**, the chosen object, and which side of the lot the street is on.
4. Move with the plain **arrow keys**, one tile at a time. **Up is north.** Each tile tells you what stands there, the walls and doors around it, when you enter another room or go outside, and whether your object fits there.
5. Press **R** to turn the object (**Shift + R** turns it the other way).
6. Press **Enter** to buy it and place it **exactly on that tile**.

If the object does not fit, the mod tells you why and where the nearest spot is that it does fit, for example "It fits 2 east, facing north". Press **Enter** again to place it there, or move to try somewhere else.

After placing, you hear which way it faces and which tiles it covers, and the cursor moves onto it, so it becomes a landmark for the next piece of furniture.

To choose a different object, press **Tab** to return to the catalog, select it, and press **Escape** to come back to placement. The new choice replaces the previous one.

## **Build Controls**

While Build placement is on:

- **Arrow keys:** Move one tile.
- **Page Up / Page Down:** Move up or down a floor.
- **Enter:** Place the chosen object here, or take the spot the mod offered.
- **R / Shift + R:** Turn the chosen object.
- **Space:** Read the tile again.
- **1:** What stands here, including anything on it and free spots on surfaces.
- **2:** Floor, and whether you are inside or outside.
- **3:** Walls and doors on all four sides.
- **4:** Objects within 3 tiles, with directions.
- **5:** The chosen object, its facing, and whether it fits here.
- **6:** Your position on the lot, the street side, and the front door.
- **Home:** Jump to the last placed object.
- **Delete:** Sell the object here. Press a second time to confirm.
- **Escape or Backspace:** Turn Build placement off.
- **Tab:** Go back to the Build catalog. In Build mode, Tab moves through the catalog in this order: filters (when a category is open), search, the Build/Buy/Function tabs, categories, then the products and their colours. EA's tool buttons such as undo are left out, because they have their own hotkeys.

The older **Ctrl + Alt** keys still work: **Ctrl + Alt + Z** undoes the last placement with a refund, and **Ctrl + Alt + left or right bracket** turns the object you last placed.

Press **F1** to return to Live Mode. This turns Build placement off. You do not need to disable Scanner mode to use the Build controls.

## **Objects on Tables & Desks**

To place a supported object on a table, desk or counter, move the cursor onto that surface first. Its free spots are read out, and objects such as lamps and computers use a free spot on it.

When objects share a tile, selling targets an object sitting on a surface before the surface itself. Selling and undo refuse objects that still contain another object or are being used. Remove their contents or finish the interaction first.

## **Undoing a Placement**

Use **Ctrl + Alt + Z** to undo a placement made with the mod and receive a refund.

⚠️ **The game's normal Build undo does not undo objects placed with the mod's cursor.** Use the mod's shortcut for those placements.

## **Current Limitations**

The mod's Build controls currently place **catalog objects**. Walls, floors, roofs, rooms, stairs, pools, terrain, and paint still require the mouse.

Objects that need a wall, ceiling, fence, water, or bare ground cannot currently be placed using the cursor. The cursor tells you when the chosen object is one of these.

An object may be unavailable because of its requirements, placement rules, or your household's funds. The game still applies those checks. If a placement fails, listen to the announcement before trying another position.
