## Editorial choices

Kept the original direct explanations, selective bold emphasis, practical
warnings, friendly tone and closing Patreon thanks. Made the scanner and
gameplay access prominent; moved detailed controls into companion posts.
Omitted obsolete shortcuts and the old limitation that narration starts
only on a loaded lot. Did not add a player-facing captions section, a
fictional support server, a source-code link or a promise of full CAS/build
access. The opening and Credits section identify tyjokr and LeonianUniverse
as joint developers, building on Screen Reader and a11yscan. The credits
also preserve the origin of the scanner and placement features. Speech Pack
remains optional for Windows fallback speech. The support section uses the
Discord invitation supplied by the user.

The TS4 Access Helper is a required setup step in the player instructions:
run it before each play session and after every Sims 4 patch. The copy
explains that it checks/enables both mod options and may need running again
if the game resets them on the first launch after a patch. It does not
promise that the helper prevents resets or updates the mod itself.
